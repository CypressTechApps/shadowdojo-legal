Privacy Policy

Shadow DojoPrivacyTermsSupport

Shadow Dojo has no account and does not collect your personal information itself.

Ads. The free version shows a full-screen ad between floors and at game over (never during a fight). Ads are served by Google AdMob. To show, limit and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, your approximate location from your IP address, and how you interact with ads, and may share them with its advertising partners. Where the law requires it, the game asks for your consent before any ad is requested, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Remove Ads. You can turn off all ads with a one-time purchase made through Google Play. Google processes the payment under its own terms and privacy policy; we receive only confirmation that the purchase was made, never your name, card or account details.

The game uses the internet only to load ads and to reach Google Play for the Remove Ads purchase. Apart from the Google Play billing permission that purchase needs, it asks for no other Android permissions.

Your settings and progress are saved only on your phone, in the game's private storage. They are never sent to us, and they are excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

We hold no data about you, so there is nothing for us to share, sell or delete. Ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences.
